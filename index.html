Tips

  • Stay under the duty-free threshold$200 for orders shipped from the US (FTA), $150 from most other countries.
  • Use an Oregon forwarding addressOregon has no state sales tax, so US orders cost less before shipping.
  • Consolidate ordersCombining packages at the forwarder before shipping to Korea usually lowers the shipping cost per item.
About

One place
to check.

Following overseas fashion brands from Korea meant checking each store for sales, new products and price changes one by one. SHOPIQ collects that in one place.

How it works:

  • Every 3 hours, a GitHub Actions job reads the public product data of 25 Shopify brand stores.
  • Price drops: each product's lowest price is compared with the previous check.
  • New in: the brand's own New Arrivals collection.
  • Sale: current price against the store's own compare-at price.
  • News: headlines from 10 publications' RSS feeds. Only headlines and links are stored.

Prices are shown in each store's currency. Always check the final price on the store before buying.
